# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: May 15, 2026*

## Overview

Sunbox is a personal morning briefing app. We collect only what we need to deliver your daily content and nothing more. We do not sell your data.

## What We Collect

- **Account information** — your name and email address when you sign up
- **Location (zip code)** — used to fetch your local weather forecast
- **Delivery preferences** — your chosen delivery time, timezone, and content selections
- **Push notification token** — used to deliver your daily Sunbox to your device

## How We Use It

Your information is used solely to personalize and deliver your daily briefing at the time you set. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond the core function of the app.

## Third-Party Services

Sunbox uses the following third-party services to fetch content:

- OpenWeatherMap (weather)
- ZenQuotes (quotes)
- Wikipedia (On This Day)
- JokeAPI (jokes)
- api.bible (Bible verses)
- API Ninjas (fun facts)
- Wordnik (word of the day)

These services receive only the minimum data needed to fulfill each request (e.g., zip code for weather). We also use Supabase for data storage and Firebase for push notification delivery.

## Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time by contacting us, and all associated data will be removed within 30 days.

## Children's Privacy

Sunbox is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from minors.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
